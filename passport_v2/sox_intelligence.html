What SOX Cannot Predict
BMS hardware failure
PCB failure and firmware crash have no electrical precursor in 30-second CAN data. The platform detects stress state — not hardware failure events.
Physical damage
Cabinet cracks, water ingress, and accident damage are invisible to CAN/GPS. Affects 23.5% of service returns.
Connector failure (pre-resistance stage)
Mechanical corrosion is invisible until it changes contact resistance. The platform detects when resistance changes — not before.
